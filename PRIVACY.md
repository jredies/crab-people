# Privacy Policy

## Overview
Crab People is a personal project that integrates fitness and recovery data from third-party services such as WHOOP and Strava.

This application is intended for private use only.

## Data Collection

This application may access the following data via authorized APIs:

- Activity data (e.g. workouts, distance, duration)
- Recovery data (e.g. sleep, HRV, strain)
- Basic account identifiers (as provided by third-party services)

No additional personal data is collected beyond what is explicitly authorized by the user.

## Data Usage

All data is used solely for:

- Personal analytics
- Generating insights via AI agents
- Improving personal training and recovery decisions

## Data Storage

Data may be stored locally or in private environments controlled by the user.  
No data is sold, shared, or distributed to third parties.

## Third-Party Services

This application relies on APIs provided by third-party services such as:

- WHOOP

Use of those services is subject to their respective privacy policies.

## Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect data, but this project is not intended for production or commercial use.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the project evolves.
